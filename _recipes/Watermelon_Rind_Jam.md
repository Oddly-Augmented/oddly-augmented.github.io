## Yield

Approximately 1 pint.

## Ingredients

| Ingredients | Amount | Unit | Note |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Watermelon rind | 4 | cups | Small dice; some pink flesh can remain |
| Apple | 1 | cup | Small dice |
| Sugar | 1 | cup |  |
| Lemon juice | 1 | whole | Fresh lemon |
| Vanilla extract | 1 | teaspoon |  |

## Instructions

1. Peel the skin from the watermelon rind and apple, then cut both into small pieces. The watermelon rind can retain some pink flesh.
2. Add all ingredients to a pot and bring to a boil.
3. Reduce the heat and simmer for 1–2 hours, until the rind is translucent.
4. Blend the jam to your desired consistency.
5. Can the jam or store it in the refrigerator.
