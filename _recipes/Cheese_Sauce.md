## Yield

22 quarts.

## Ingredients

| Ingredients | Amount | Unit | Note |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Butter | 2 | lb |  |
| Flour | 500 | grams |  |
| Milk | 2 | gal |  |
| Heavy cream | 3.5 | qt |  |
| Cheddar cheese | 7 | lb | Shredded |
| Muenster cheese | 3 | lb | Shredded |
| Mascarpone | 2.5 | lb |  |
| Salt | 150 | grams |  |

## Instructions

1. Heat the milk, heavy cream, and salt in a 300°F oven until scalding, about 175°F.
2. Melt the butter in a rondeau over medium heat.
3. Add the flour, stirring constantly, until it reaches a blonde color and smells nutty.
4. Slowly add the hot milk mixture in small additions, stirring until fully incorporated after each addition.
5. Add the Muenster and cheddar cheeses in three batches, stirring after each addition until melted.
6. Add the mascarpone.
7. Blend with an immersion blender until completely smooth.
