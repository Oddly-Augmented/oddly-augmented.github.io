## Ingredients

- 4–5 small cucumbers
- 4 cloves garlic, smashed
- A few sprigs of fresh dill
- 2 cups water
- 1.5 tbsp salt
- Optional spices (peppercorns, mustard seeds, etc.)

## Instructions

1. Pack cucumbers, garlic, dill, and spices into a clean jar.
2. Mix water and salt until dissolved, then pour over the cucumbers.
3. Keep everything submerged (use a weight if needed).
4. Let ferment at room temperature for 3–7 days, checking daily.
5. Taste, then move to the fridge when they’re as sour as you like.

## Notes

- Keep the jar loosely covered so gas can escape.
- If anything looks or smells off, trust your senses and toss it.
- Write down what you changed (spices, time, temperature) so you can tweak future batches.
