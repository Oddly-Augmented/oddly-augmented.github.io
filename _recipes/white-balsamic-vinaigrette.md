## Ingredients

| Ingredients | Amount | Unit |
|---|---:|---|
| White balsamic vinegar | 2 | cups |
| Dijon | 1 | cup |
| Honey | 1/2 | cup |
| Dried oregano | 1 | tbsp |
| Extra-virgin olive oil (EVOO) | 2 | cups |
| Salad oil | 1 | qt |
| Salt | 2 | tsp |
| Black pepper | 1 | tsp |

## Instructions

1. Add all ingredients to a Vitamix.
2. Blend until fully combined and emulsified.
